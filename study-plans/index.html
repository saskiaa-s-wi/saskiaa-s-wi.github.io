Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026. This page is written in plain words on purpose.

The short version

  • We only save what the app needs to work: your sign-in details, your username, and the notes and files you upload.
  • Your private notes are for you only. Your public notes can be seen by every student who is signed in.
  • You can delete any of your notes at any time, or delete everything we hold about you with one button in Settings.
  • We do not sell your information, and this app has no ads and no tracking tools.

What we save

  • Your sign-in: your email address and password if you sign up with email, or the email address GitHub shares with us if you use GitHub. Passwords are scrambled when they are saved, so nobody can read them, including us. We never see your GitHub password.
  • Your profile: your username, your role (regular user, moderator, or admin), and your light or dark mode choice.
  • Your notes: the class title, topic or unit, description, the file you upload (its name, size, and the file itself), whether it is public or private, and when you added it.
  • Reports: if you report a post or a user, we save who you are, what you reported, the reason you gave, and when you sent it.
  • In your browser: a sign-in token that keeps you signed in on this device (signing out removes it), and a note of your last light or dark mode choice so the page looks right when it opens.

Your data is stored with Supabase, the service that runs this app's database and file storage. GitHub is used if you choose to sign in with GitHub, and this site is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Who can see it

What Who can see it
Your email address Not shown to other students.
Your password Nobody. It is saved in scrambled form.
Your username Every signed-in student. It is shown on your public notes.
Public notes and their files Every signed-in student. Moderators and the admin can also remove them. People who are not signed in cannot see them.
Private notes and their files Only you.
Reports you send Moderators and the admin only. The person you reported is not shown who reported them.

Links to files stop working after one hour. If you copy a file link and share it, anyone who has it can open that file until it expires, so only share links you are happy for others to see.

The person who runs this app manages the database and file storage, so they can reach everything saved in them. They only look when needed to run the app, handle a report, or fix a problem.

How to delete your data

  • Delete a note and its file: go to the Dashboard, find your note under "Public Files & Notes" or "My Private Files & Notes", click Delete, and confirm. The note and its file are removed for good.
  • Remove your sign-in from this device: click Sign Out.
  • Change your password: go to Settings, then Change Password (email accounts only).
  • Delete everything: go to Settings, click Delete my data, type DELETE, and confirm. This removes your account, username, notes, files, and the reports you sent. It happens straight away and cannot be undone. Reports other people sent about you may be kept for a while so moderators can finish reviewing them.
